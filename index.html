Gotcha.

You just scanned a QR code from a stranger. At a cyber security talk.

Relax, nothing happened. This page collects nothing and tracks nothing. There is also no espresso machine. Sorry about that.

A real attacker's page would have
  • asked you to “confirm” your work login
  • pushed a “security update” to install
  • sent you to a fake parking or parcel payment page

> attack_type: quishing (QR code phishing)

Three habits that beat it
  • Read the link preview before you tap it.
  • Never sign in to anything right after scanning a code.
  • When in doubt, type the address yourself.
